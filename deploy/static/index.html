Features

Designed around how people actually talk.

Anchor isn't a chatbot dressed up in therapy clothes. It's built for the way real conversations actually move. From the small stuff to the heavy stuff.

Listens, doesn't lecture

No instant pep talks, no scripted "I hear you." Anchor reads tone and meets you where you actually are.

Remembers what matters

Your name, the people in your life, what actually helps. Carried across conversations and stored only on your device.

Lives on your phone

The full model runs locally on iOS and Android. No internet required, no conversation logs sent anywhere.

Safe by default

Trained to catch crisis cues and point you to real help. Anchor knows when to step aside.

Checks in on you

Anchor doesn't wait for you to start. It follows up on the hard conversation, the review on Friday, how you slept. It remembers so you don't have to re-explain.

Built to be honest

Not a replacement for therapy, and Anchor will be upfront about that. A companion, not a clinician.

Questions

Things people ask.

Is Anchor a therapist?

No. Anchor is a companion. It listens and responds thoughtfully, but it isn't a clinician, can't diagnose, and isn't a substitute for professional care. If you're in crisis, it will point you to real resources.

Where do my conversations go?

On the iOS and Android app, conversations never leave your device. On this web preview, messages pass through our server to generate responses and are logged in anonymised form only to improve the model. Never sold, never shared.

How is this different from ChatGPT?

Anchor is a small model fine-tuned specifically for wellness conversations, built to run on your phone. Purpose-built. Warmer by design. Fully private.

How much does it cost?

The web preview is free. The app will have a free tier at launch, with a subscription for advanced features down the line. No surprise paywalls.
