About Anchor

What is Anchor?

Anchor is a private AI mental wellness companion built to give you a safe space to talk through what's on your mind. It's not a replacement for therapy — it's a thoughtful presence that listens, remembers, and responds with care.

This is an early web preview. The full version runs entirely on your phone with no internet connection required, so your conversations never leave your device.

How memory works

When you fill in your Profile tab, that information is saved to your browser's local storage and included as context when you chat, so Anchor can respond more personally without you re-explaining yourself each time.

Your conversations pass through our server to generate responses and are logged in anonymised form (emails and phone numbers are scrubbed). Logs are used solely to improve the model — they are never sold or shared with third parties.

The model

Anchor is powered by a fine-tuned version of Llama 3.2 3B, trained on mental wellness conversations with a focus on empathetic, non-clinical support. The model is quantised (Q4_K_M) and optimised to run on consumer hardware — including fully on-device on Android.

Important disclaimer

Anchor is not a licensed therapist, counsellor, or medical professional. It cannot diagnose, treat, or provide clinical mental health advice. If you are in crisis or need immediate help, please reach out to a professional.
